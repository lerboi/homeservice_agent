Phase 61.1 is a clean, well-scoped regression-fix delivery. The three fixes (prompt reframe, WR-01, WR-02) and the two SKILL.md updates are correctly implemented and well-tested.

**Prompt reframe:** The deadlock fix is sound. Pre-tool readback is explicitly licensed. The "Silence is always acceptable" escape hatch is removed in both EN and ES. Re-introducing hallucination risk is unlikely — the verdict-token gating is unchanged and the prohibited-phrase list is intact.

The wording is symmetric between the two tools and stays under the 1024-char Pitfall A6 budget.

The log line preserves D-C2' "we know why this row is missing" semantics.
