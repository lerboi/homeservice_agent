### Lesson learned — slot fabrication as a confirmation question is its own failure class

Phase 61's existing OUTCOME WORDS rule already prohibited fabricated
slot times. It did not fire for incident `AJ_W7woUYGhG5CV` because the
agent's failure mode was to fabricate a time AS A CONFIRMATION QUESTION
(*"so just to confirm, you're available on Thursday at 10 AM?"*) — not
as a confirmation claim ("Thursday at 10 AM is booked"), not as a filler
("let me check 10 AM for you"), and not as an availability assertion
("Thursday at 10 AM is available"). It fell in the seam between
TOOL NARRATION's filler-scoped slot-time prohibition and OUTCOME WORDS'
confirmation-language-scoped slot-time prohibition.

The fix is a new dedicated CRITICAL RULE that scopes the prohibition to
the conversational moment the caller has not yet given a time, NOT to a
particular surface (filler vs confirmation vs assertion). The
load-bearing clause is `"This includes proposing it as a confirmation
question"` — that is the substring that catches the incident failure
mode.

This builds on the Phase 61.1 lesson (`feedback_directive_prompt_silence_deadlock.md`):
scope every CRITICAL RULE to the moment it governs, never license
silence in info-gathering loops, and prefer non-directive phrasing
(`feedback_livekit_prompt_philosophy.md`). Phase 61.2's Fix 1 (URGENCY
trigger) honors both — no silence license, non-directive ("you have
what you need to check availability"), scoped to the moment all four
trigger fields are gathered.
