# How Voco works

## The three steps of a call
1. The call comes in, on the phone line or over the web. Voco answers at once.
2. Voco understands what the caller wants, asks for anything missing, and decides on the action.
3. The action runs in the business's system: the appointment is booked, the record created, the task assigned. The caller is told it is done.

## Getting set up
Setup is done with the Voco team, not self-serve:
1. Book a demo and tell us what callers ask for and which systems hold the answers.
2. We configure the agent and connect those systems, with the access the business approves.
3. We test it together on real calls against the business's own data.
4. It goes live on the calls the business chooses: all calls, only out of hours, or only when nobody picks up.

The business does not have to build anything.

## Phone number
Calls reach Voco on a dedicated number that the business's existing line forwards to.
