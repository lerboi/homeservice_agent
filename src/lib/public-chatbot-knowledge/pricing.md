# Voco pricing

Voco does not publish a price table. Pricing is scoped with each business because it depends on three things:

1. Call volume: how many calls the agent takes in a month and how long they run.
2. Systems connected: a calendar is simpler than a CRM plus the business's own API.
3. What the agent does: booking only, or booking plus intake, lookups, callbacks and escalation.

Every setup includes: setup and testing with the Voco team, the connections to the business's systems, a dedicated phone number, transcripts and recordings of every call, and changes to rules and wording over time.

Do not quote any dollar amount, plan name, call allowance or trial length. If asked for a number, say that it depends on the above and that the team will give one after a short conversation.

Existing customers can see their plan and usage in their dashboard under Billing after signing in.
