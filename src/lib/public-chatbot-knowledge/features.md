# What Voco does

## On the call
- Answers on the business's phone line, in the business's name.
- Understands requests in the caller's own words; there is no menu or keypad.
- Replies in the language the caller is speaking.
- Typically starts speaking 1 to 1.5 seconds after the caller stops.
- Reads important details back and waits for a yes before acting.
- Hands off to a person when the caller asks or the business's rules say so.

## In the business's systems
- Appointment booking: checks real availability and writes the booking to the calendar. A slot is reserved in one atomic step, so two callers cannot take the same time.
- Lead intake: creates the contact and the lead in the CRM with the conversation attached.
- Tasks and callbacks: creates a follow-up for the right team, with a due time.
- Order and account lookup: finds the caller's record and reads back its status.
- After-hours coverage: the same work at night, at weekends and when lines are busy.

More detail: [Use cases](/use-cases)

## After the call
- A transcript and a recording of every call, with the actions the agent took.
- Confirmations to the caller by text message or email.
- Alerts to the team for calls that need a person.

## Control
- The business sets the rules: working hours, what can be booked and when, what the agent may and may not do, when to escalate and to whom.
- The agent only tells a caller something is done after the system confirms it.
