# Voco Agent

## What it is

Voco Agent is an AI agent connected to the systems a business already runs: reviews and customer feedback, the email inbox, the calendar, search data, the support desk and the business's own records. The owner or the team messages it the way they would message a colleague. It finds the answer in those systems, then does the job it is asked to do.

It is one of Voco's two products. The other, Voco Voice, answers phone and web calls. A business can take either or both.

## What it does

- Customer feedback: reads reviews, survey answers and complaints, groups what people keep saying, and replies to them.
- Being found on Google: watches where the business appears in search and what people looked for, and explains changes in plain words.
- Email: sorts the inbox, drafts replies, chases the conversations that went quiet.
- Calendar: books, moves and cancels, and tells the people affected.
- Customer support: answers routine questions from the business's records, opens and updates tickets, hands over the hard ones.
- The weekly picture: a regular summary of what happened, what changed and what it suggests.

It both reports and acts. The business decides which jobs it may do on its own and which it prepares and waits for an OK on.

## Where you talk to it

WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or email. There is no new app to learn.

## Control

- The business sets what it may do alone and what needs approval, job by job.
- Each answer shows where it came from and each action is logged.
- It works only in the accounts the business agrees to connect.

## How a business gets it

The same way as Voco Voice: a demo, then a setup done by the Voco team, who connect the systems, set the rules with the business and test it on real questions. There is no self-serve signup.
