# Calls — Voco Dashboard

## What this section does
The Calls section is your single phone surface. It opens on the call log — every call handled by your AI receptionist, grouped by date with caller number, duration, outcome, urgency label, recording, and transcript.

When callers are waiting for a callback, a highlighted banner appears at the top of the page: "**N callers waiting for a callback**". Click **View** on the banner to open the **Callbacks** view — your work queue of callers who didn't book. Tap their number to call back, then convert them to a Job or mark them lost. Use the **← All calls** control at the top of the Callbacks view to return to the call log.

## Key features
- **Callbacks banner** — appears above the stats only when someone is waiting for a callback
- **Callbacks queue** — callbacks waiting on you, with tap-to-call numbers; Open / Booked / Lost sub-filters keep handled history reachable
- **Call log** — full history of all calls, grouped by date
- **Expandable cards** — click any call to expand and see recording player and transcript
- **Triage labels** — emergency, urgent, or routine labels set by the AI based on call content
- **Duration and outcome** — see call length and whether the AI booked, captured a job, or escalated
- **Filters** — in the call log, filter by outcome, urgency label, or date range

## How to navigate here
Go to [Calls](/dashboard/calls). To jump straight to the callback queue, use [Callbacks](/dashboard/calls?view=callbacks).

## Common tasks

### How do I call someone back?
Open [Callbacks](/dashboard/calls?view=callbacks) and tap the phone number on any card to call. After you've spoken, open the card's detail flyout and click **Convert to Job** (if you booked them) or **Mark as Lost** (if they went elsewhere).

### Where did the Inquiries tab go?
It merged into Calls. Everything that used to live on the Inquiries tab is now the **Callbacks** view of the Calls page — same callbacks, same convert and mark-lost actions. Open it from the banner at the top of Calls or directly at [Callbacks](/dashboard/calls?view=callbacks).

### How do I see callbacks I already handled?
In the Callbacks view, use the **Booked** and **Lost** pills to see callbacks you converted or closed.

### How do I listen to a call recording?
On the Calls page, click the call card to expand it. The audio player appears inline. Click play to listen to the full recording.

### How do I read a call transcript?
Expand the call card — the transcript appears below the audio player, showing the full conversation between the AI and the caller.

### What do the triage labels mean?
- **Emergency** — Immediate safety risk or urgent issue (gas leak, flooding, no heat in winter)
- **High-ticket** — Job likely worth $500+, complex install or replacement
- **Routine** — Standard service request, future scheduling, or quote inquiry

### How do I find calls from a specific number?
On the Calls page, use the search bar or date filter at the top to locate calls from a specific name, phone number, or date range.
